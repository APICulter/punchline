How to play

One big screen for the questions, one phone per player.

  1. Create a game on the big screen. Choose 1 to 10 rounds, or enter a premium code for extra questions.
  2. Everyone opens Punchline on their phone, types the PIN and picks a name.
  3. A question appears. Write your answer before the clock runs out: the host sets how long you get.
  4. The answers show up on every phone, without names. Vote for your favourite, just not your own.
  5. Every vote you get is worth 1 point. Most points after the last round wins.
